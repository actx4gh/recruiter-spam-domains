# Recruiter / Job Spam Domains

Manually curated domains observed in unsolicited recruiter or job-spam email based on firsthand recipient experience.

## Scope

This repository is for inbox-defense and filtering use.

A listed domain may be:
- a direct recruiter or staffing domain
- mail, ATS, CRM, tracking, or campaign infrastructure materially used in those mail flows

Inclusion is not a legal claim of fraud, criminality, or business illegitimacy.

## Formats

- `domains.txt` — plain one-domain-per-line list
- `adblock.txt` — Adblock-style domain rules
- `hosts.txt` — hosts-file format

## Inclusion standard

Add a domain only when there is repeated firsthand evidence that it appears in unwanted recruiter or job-spam email as a sender, relay, bounce, tracking, or campaign domain.

Remove a domain when it was added in error, the evidence is too weak, or the collateral blocking is too broad.

## Privacy

Do not post unredacted personal data, full email bodies, or full raw headers in issues or pull requests.
